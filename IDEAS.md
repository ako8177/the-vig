## Charms and the skill tree (2026-10-14, to work out with the owner: DECISIONS #84)

Markers now buy charms only. Every charm must touch two systems (pillar 3). Names and numbers are
proposals, ours, not from CloverPit.

**The charms today**: Lucky Fuse (win a duel after a shock: +1 life), Overclock (survive a shock:
stakes and wins x2 for the next night's first 60 s), Lead Apron (the first life you'd lose each
night costs nothing), Marker Magnet (double markers from high tables and dead levers), Insulated
Kit (locked: kill the Collector; two free wire traces a duel, Sticky Floor can't slow you).

**Charm ideas**
- Grifter's Gloves: see the 21 dealer's hole card once a night; in the duel, swap two levers' wires once.
- Hedge Chip: a lost low-table bet returns 20%; you may re-spin the Bane wheel once.
- Night Owl: the night clock gets +30 s; your marks wear off 25% faster.
- House Comp Card: one free drink a night; drinks' edges +2%.
- Counting Card: 21's panel shows the shoe's running count; Red Line's deck shows how many reds are left.
- Hot Hand: three wins in a row: the next payout +25%; a win clears one Hi-Lo mark stack.
- Mourning Band (locked: the Auditor): after a kill, next night's quota -15%; the next Collector starts with a shock.
- Spare Fuse (locked: the Receiver): you can take three shocks in a duel; Red Line's streak needs four losses.
- Ledger Pen (locked: the Auditor): overshoot pays a marker per $400, not $500; see tomorrow's quota at night start.

**Skill tree ideas** (a currency to pick: a skill point per night survived and per Collector
killed, kept between runs)
- The Gambler (tables): Short Memory (Hi-Lo marks 20 s), Table Limits (higher max bets), Shoe Reader (21 shows the count), Steady Hands (the Bandit's grid grows to 3x4).
- The Survivor (lives): +1 starting life, Thick Skin (your first shock in each duel doesn't count), Second Wind (a ghost may buy themself back at 75%).
- The Hustler (money): Float (+$500 bank every night), Fourth Slot (a fourth charm slot), Haggler (charms cost 1 marker less), Express Post (orders arrive at once).
- The Regular (the house and the bar): drinks last longer, a comped first drink, the family leaves a gift at the door on night 2.
